# Print Partner lane

The lane procedure lives in one place for every runner: [.claude/skills/pp-lane/SKILL.md](../../../.claude/skills/pp-lane/SKILL.md). Read it and follow it. It covers start checks, the reading list, the fast and full local sets, the standing rebase rule, no stacking, evidence, review, push, the PR body, merging or reporting readiness, and cleaning `rust/target`.
